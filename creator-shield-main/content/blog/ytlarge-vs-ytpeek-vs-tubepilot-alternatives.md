## YTLarge, YTPeek, and TubePilot: The Basic Checkers

Tools like YTLarge, YTPeek, and TubePilot were built for an older version of YouTube. They are essentially **public API scrapers**. 

When you plug your channel or video URL into these tools, they perform a few basic functions:
1.  **Index Checking:** They check if your video shows up when its exact title is searched.
2.  **Ratio Analysis:** They look at your public view-to-like ratios or comment sentiment.
3.  **Country Restrictions:** They check if your video is geographically blocked.

**The Problem:** None of these metrics diagnose a modern algorithmic shadowban. YouTube does not publicly broadcast shadowbans through its API. Just because a tool like YTLarge confirms your video is "Indexed," it does *not* mean the algorithm is recommending it on the Home Page.

## Why Basic Checkers Fail in 2026

The YouTube algorithm no longer relies on basic tags and metadata. Today, the algorithm analyzes your raw video file to calculate an **"Originality Score."**

If your video contains synthetic AI voiceovers without human breath variance, or if your script is 80% semantically similar to 50 other videos in your niche, the algorithm flags it for "Repetitious Content." It quietly suppresses your impressions without ever notifying you or changing your public API status.

Because YTLarge, YTPeek, and TubePilot do not download and analyze your raw audio and video vectors, **they cannot see these algorithmic penalties.** They will tell you your channel is perfectly healthy while your views remain stuck at zero.
